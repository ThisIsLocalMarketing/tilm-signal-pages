# Community Card Operating Standard

## Issue schedule

Every TILM Community Card is assigned an issue month and scheduled to ship and reach mailboxes by the first Wednesday of that month.

- Build the printer, proof, payment, route, and postal schedule backward from the first-Wednesday mailbox target.
- Treat the first Wednesday as a planning target, not a guaranteed postal delivery date.
- Keep the public placement mix flexible. The internal working model of 11 Standard and 6 Featured placements, with an optional Anchor, is a revenue-planning assumption—not fixed card capacity, promised public inventory, or a print-readiness trigger.
- If print readiness cannot be reached inside the verified printer and postal window, move the card to the next issue month rather than rushing an unapproved or underfunded issue.
- Print readiness is reached only when cleared, proof-approved revenue covers the verified all-in shipped-card cost, final artwork is ready, and printer and postal specifications are approved. It is not tied to a fixed sold-slot count.

## QR measurement

TILM-managed Community Card QR paths use the approved tracking service before opening the resident destination.

Private aggregate reporting may include:

- total scan events,
- card side, placement, or campaign attribution,
- mobile, tablet, desktop, or unknown device class,
- approved advertiser action clicks,
- action type,
- scan-to-action rate.

The report helps a business owner understand which card side, prompt, offer, or next step attracted attention and gives TILM evidence for improving later creative and issue planning.

## Measurement boundaries and privacy

- A scan or action click is not a completed call, booking, visit, lead, sale, redemption, revenue, or ROI.
- Do not store IP addresses.
- Do not store full referrer URLs; retain only the hostname when available.
- A short first-party source cookie may connect a scan to a later tracked action.
- Keep advertiser reports private.
- Publish a clear measurement notice anywhere a tracked card QR sends residents.

## Before print

- Confirm the issue month and first-Wednesday mailbox target.
- Confirm the verified printer and postal workback schedule.
- Seed and verify final QR destinations and advertiser action paths.
- Confirm the public measurement notice matches the data actually collected.
- Decode and scan-test the final QR masters and the physical printer proof.
- Name the person responsible for post-launch reporting and resident-page updates.
