Important questions

What business owners usually need to know.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo, business contact or preferred action, one offer or message, and any useful photos or brand assets you already have. TILM uses that material to shape the proposed placement for your approval.

What happens after someone scans?

The QR code opens the approved page or business action. TILM reports total scans and tracked button clicks so the business can see what gets attention and improve future offers. Reports are aggregate, do not include IP addresses, and show activity—not completed sales or bookings.

When is each card planned to arrive?

TILM schedules each issue to ship and reach mailboxes by the first Wednesday of its issue month. The founding plan remains 5,000 cards with Sutton homes first. The first Wednesday is the operating target—not a guaranteed postal delivery date—and final quantity and timing remain subject to print readiness, printer confirmation, and postal conditions.

Are calls, scans, or sales guaranteed?

No. The product provides a coordinated local visibility opportunity and a clear action path. Calls, scans, visits, sales, redemptions, revenue, and other results vary and are not guaranteed.
